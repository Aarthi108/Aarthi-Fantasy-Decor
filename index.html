About Us

Hi, I'm Aarthi. Decorating is my passion.

Creating beautiful things has been part of me since childhood. I have always loved turning simple materials into something magical, whether it is a bunch of balloons, a handful of flowers or a corner that needs a little more colour. That love is exactly what Aarthi's Fantasy Decor is built on.

I have been bringing that passion to celebrations for nearly six years now. Every event teaches me something new, and seeing people smile when they walk into a space I have decorated is the reward I value most.

My style: detailed, colourful and personal. I am very keen on the small things, from how balloons are layered to how a floral piece is finished, because those details are what make a celebration feel special.

What makes us different: I work hard to understand exactly what each client is asking for. I show you a design mock-up and visualisation first, and I only move forward once you are happy with the plan. Your satisfaction matters more to me than anything else in this business.
